# Specialisations 

| Category        | Effect                                                                                                              |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Legendary/tarot | Whenever you sell a legendary item, you may move one card used to create it to the top of the deck.                 |
| Legendary/tarot | After using a Mythic Shrine of Discovered Potential, you may reject the outcome, refunding half of the tokens used. |
| Soul/reliquary  | Mythic Shrines of Annexation add modifiers until a reliquary has 4 modifiers (instead of 3).                        |
| Soul/reliquary  | The proc chance of your souls is increased by 1% for each soul in the party (regardless of presence).               |
| Misc            | The chance of obtaining special loot results is doubled.                                                            |
