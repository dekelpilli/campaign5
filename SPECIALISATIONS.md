# Specialisations

| Name         | Category        | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Recycler     | Legendary/tarot | Whenever you sell a legendary item, you may move one card used to create it to the top of the deck.                                                                                                         |
| Seeker       | Legendary/tarot | When using a Shrine of Discovered Potential, you receive two offers and may choose one of them.                                                                                                             |
| All-in       | Legendary/tarot | You may turn in 3 cards instead of 2 to create a legendary item. Their sell price scales accordingly.                                                                                                       |
| Hedger       | Legendary/tarot | Whenever you draw a tarot card, draw one additional card and choose one of the two. Shuffle the other one back into the deck.                                                                               |
| Favourite    | Ring            | You can ignore the point cost on one of your rings.                                                                                                                                                         |
| Forager      | Ring            | Whenever you find rings, you may choose two rings out of three (instead of just receiving two rings).                                                                                                       |
| Jeweller     | Ring            | Shrines of Forging cost 40 tokens instead of 50. The sell price of rings affected by Shrines of Forging reduces accordingly.                                                                                |
| Bare         | Ring            | Shrines of Forging can reduce a ring's cost by one more than usual.                                                                                                                                         |
| Overcharged  | Soul/reliquary  | Shrines of Annexation add modifiers until a reliquary has 4 modifiers (instead of 3).                                                                                                                       |
| Overloaded   | Soul/reliquary  | Your reliquaries may contain up to 4 souls (instead of 3).                                                                                                                                                  |
| Dissociative | Soul/reliquary  | You can merge two souls that have been targeted by a Fulfilment shrine at least 4 times (each). The souls are destroyed, but create a new Soul with your choice of Passive and Proc mods from those souls.  |
| Intentional  | Soul/reliquary  | Whenever you find a soul, also gain an itemised reliquary mod. You can sell it for 5 soul tokens, or consume it to guarantee its mod will be added the next time you use a Refinement or Annexation shrine. |
| Galvanised   | Tattoo          | You can equip 4 runes, instead of 3 (and therefore may have four inspirations).                                                                                                                             |
| Substantive  | Tattoo          | Your runes can have up to 5 mods, instead of 4.                                                                                                                                                             |
| Vacillator   | Tattoo          | Shrines of Reimagining are free, and Shrines of Inspiration are free when you already have the maximum number of inspirations.                                                                              |
| Slavager     | Tattoo          | When selling one-mod, un-upgraded runes, also gain 25 tokens that can only be used for shrines targeting runes.                                                                                             |
| Insatiable   | Vestige         | The maximum level of vestiges is 6 instead of 5.                                                                                                                                                            |
| Picky        | Vestige         | All vestige boons are revealed. When you level up a vestige, you may level it up twice, but neither levels grant any boons.                                                                                 |
| Creative     | Vestige         | For every 10 boons gained, a player may come up with one theme/archetype for which a new vestige type will be created.                                                                                      |
| Selective    | Vestige         | You may convert a level 1 vestige to any other vestige depiction, at no cost.                                                                                                                               |
| Gambler      | Miscellanious   | The chance of obtaining special loot results is doubled.                                                                                                                                                    |
| Compensated  | Miscellanious   | When rejecting the result of a Shrine, refund 20% of the tokens used (stacking with other refunding machanics).                                                                                             |
