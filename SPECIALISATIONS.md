# Specialisations

| Name         | Category        | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Recycler     | Legendary/tarot | Whenever you sell a legendary item, you may move one card used to create it to the top of the deck.                                                                                                         |
| Seeker       | Legendary/tarot | When using a Shrine of Discovered Potential, you receive two offers and may choose one of them.                                                                                                             |
| All-in       | Legendary/tarot | You may turn in 3 cards instead of 2 to create a legendary item. Their sell price scales accordingly.                                                                                                       |
| Hedger       | Legendary/tarot | Whenever you draw a tarot card, draw one additional card and choose one of the two.                                                                                                                         |
| Overcharged  | Soul/reliquary  | Shrines of Annexation add modifiers until a reliquary has 4 modifiers (instead of 3).                                                                                                                       |
| Overloaded   | Soul/reliquary  | Your reliquaries may contain up to 4 souls (instead of 3).                                                                                                                                                  |
| Dissociative | Soul/reliquary  | You can merge two souls that have been targeted by a Fulfilment shrine at least 4 times. The souls are destroyed, but create a new Soul with your choice of Passive and Proc mods from those souls.         |
| Intentional  | Soul/reliquary  | Whenever you find a soul, also gain an itemised reliquary mod. You can sell it for 5 soul tokens, or consume it to guarantee its mod will be added the next time you use a Refinement or Annexation shrine. |
| Insatiable   | Vestige         | The maximum level of vestiges is 6 instead of 5.                                                                                                                                                            |
| Picky        | Vestige         | All vestige boons are revealed. When you level up a vestige, you may level it up twice, but neither levels grant any boons.                                                                                 |
| Creative     | Vestige         | For every 10 boons gained, a player may come up with one theme/archetype for which a new vestige type will be created.                                                                                      |
| Selective    | Vestige         | You may convert a level 1 vestige to any other vestige depiction, at no cost.                                                                                                                               |
