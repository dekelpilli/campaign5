# Campaign Loot

The loot economy for a Spies and Spiders campaign (a TTRPG derived from D&D; rule differences: https://homebrewery.naturalcrit.com/share/Zbg0uhdcWAVM, full content: https://spies.tools): what players find, how it is upgraded, and the currency that drives upgrades.

## Language

### Mods

**Mod**:
A single effect carried by a piece of loot. Short for modifier.

**Affinity**:
The category of effect a mod provides (e.g. offence, survivability, meta). A mod may have several.
_Avoid_: Tag

**Downside Mod**:
A mod with no affinity, acting as a drawback on its item.

**Rank**:
An individual mod's progression step, raised independently of the item carrying it.

### Obtaining loot

**Loot Roll**:
An entitlement a player finds or buys, redeemed by rolling 1d100 to receive a random loot result.

**Loot Result**:
What a player receives from redeeming a loot roll; may contain more than one thing.

**Special Loot Result**:
A loot result from the top of the d100 range: either a choice of any loot type, or a reroll plus a mission.

**Loot Type**:
A category of regular loot result: ring, soul, tarot card, tattoo, or vestige.

**Mission**:
A goal granted alongside other loot that rewards loot on completion. Not a loot type.

### Shrines & currency

**Shrine**:
A paid upgrade that improves an item, costed in shrine tokens of specific token types.

**Shrine Token**:
The currency spent at shrines, gained by selling loot.

### Souls & reliquaries

**Reliquary**:
A container that holds a player's souls and may carry its own modifiers.

**Soul**:
A socketable loot item providing a passive bonus and a proc effect. Souls and reliquaries share the soul token type.

### Legendary items

**Legendary Item**:
A named item with a pre-defined set of mods, created by a turn-in. A player holds at most two.
_Avoid_: Relic

**Tarot Card**:
A card turned in alongside others to create a legendary item, each altering the item it creates. Shares the legendary token type.

**Turn-in**:
Exchanging a set of tarot cards for a new legendary item.

**Inherent Mods**:
The mods a legendary item starts with. Tarot cards may remove or add to them (e.g. Death swaps in another legendary's downsides).

**Native Mod**:
An inherent mod from the legendary's own default set that survived tarot effects.

**Signature Mod**:
The most distinctive inherent mod of a legendary, not necessarily its strongest.

**Legendary Level**:
A legendary item's progression step, which strengthens its inherent mods; raised by the Shrine of Revealed Potential.

**Discoverable Mod**:
A mod in a legendary item's unique, initially hidden pool, from which discovered mods are drawn.

**Discovered Mod**:
A discoverable mod that has been added to a legendary item. Unaffected by legendary level; progresses through its own rank.
_Avoid_: Infused modifier, infusion

### Vestiges

**Boon**:
A mod granted by a vestige.

**Tier**:
A vestige's linear progression step, each unlocking an additional set of boons; raised by the Shrine of Bestowing. Distinct from a legendary level.
