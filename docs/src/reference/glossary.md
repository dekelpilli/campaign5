# Glossary

Let me know if you come across anything that's unclear, 
or encounter terms/keywords that don't mean much to you, and I'll add them below.

| Term       | Meaning                                                                                                                                     |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Absorption | How much damage is absorbed from an incoming attack. Absorbed damage is resisted, and an equal amount is healed before the damage is taken. |
