# Shrines

- Shrines can be used to upgrade and modify your gear (and even your missions).
- You can buy uses of a shrine for tokens in town.
- You can reject the outcome of a shrine, instead choosing to keep the item the same as it was beforehand.
  - By default, you are not refunded or compensated in any way when you do this,
but more luck-dependent shrines are cheaper (relative to their potential power level).
