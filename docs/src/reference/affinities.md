# Affinities

Each (non-downside) mod on your items will have at least one affinity.
These affinities are just categories that describe what the mod provides. They have no effect on their own,
but there are mods that can interact with other mods based on their affinities.

## Types
A mod can have one or more of the following affinities:
- Offence (damage, harm, crit, accuracy)
- Survivability (defence/hp/regen/incoming healing)
- Tactics (avoiding control conditions, initiative, movement, improved uptime/usability of abilities (range, reach, etc), disengage)
- Resource (reduce resource usage/improve resource recovery)
- Control (lower enemy Offence/Tactics)
- Support (outgoing healing, allies/area benefits, lowering enemy Survivability)
- Utility (skills, out of combat benefits)
- Meta (gold/token improvements, XP, impact on other loot/mods)
