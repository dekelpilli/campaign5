# Rings

- Rings are items that grant bonuses to the character, often with conditions or scaling based on your other equipped rings.
- A character cannot equip more than 50 points worth of rings. There is no limit on how many rings a character can equip,
so long as the point total is 50 or lower.
- You cannot improve the bonuses granted by rings via shrines. Instead, a _Shrine of Forging_ can be used to reduce the cost of a ring,
letting you fit more rings and scale existing ones better.
- Rings have a predefined material (diamond, gold, silver, or opal), and a type (e.g. lion, elephant, dolphin). 
These have no intrinsic effect, but can be referenced by rings to cause synergies or counter-synergies.
