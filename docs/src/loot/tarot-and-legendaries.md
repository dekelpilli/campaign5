# Tarot & Legendary Items

## Creating legendary items
Legendary items are created by 'turning in' a set of 2 tarot cards. A random legendary item is generated, and then it 
is modified by the cards in the turn in.

You may equip a maximum of two legendary items at one time.

## Upgrading legendary items
Legendary items can be upgraded in two ways:
1. Increasing the 'level' of the item (_Shrine of Revealed Potential_). Each legendary item has all of its levels pre-determined, 
so upgrading it in this way provides an RNG-free way to improve your item.
2. Add a new modifier to the item, or level up another modifier added in this way (_Shrine of Discovered Potential_).
Each legendary item has a set of pre-determined 'discoverable' mods, and this shrine either adds these mods to the item, 
or ranks up such a mod already on the item.

Note that some cards might change how upgrading your item works, or which mods are present/available on the item.
