# Runes

- Runes are a loot type that grants bonuses specific to your character.
- An 'inspiration' for a rune is an aspect of your character (class/subclass feature, knack, feat, spell/maneuver) that is
used as a basis for a rune. When a rune has this as its inspiration, all of its mods will either impact this inspiration,
or grant bonuses that build on the bonuses it grants.
- You can equip up to three runes at a time, and can have up to three inspirations at a time. 
You cannot equip more than one rune of the same inspiration.
- If you find a rune with the same inspiration as one you already have, you can either merge it into your existing rune, 
or simply sell it.
- You start the campaign with one source of inspiration, chosen by the GM.
  A _Shrine of Inspiration_ allows you to choose a new inspiration. The shrine can also replace an inspiration you have chosen
  (e.g. if you've decided to unlearn a spell), if you are already at your maximum inspirations.
  - This takes effect at the start of the next session, and the new inspiration needs to be designed/created.
- Runes are upgraded in two main ways:
  - _Shrine of Weaving_: add a random mod onto the rune (up to 4, from a pool of 4)
  - _Shrine of Honing_: once a rune has 4 mods on it, you can start upgrading the mods themselves with this shrine
- If you don't like a mod for one of your runes, you can remove it and have it replaced with a new one via a _Shrine of Reimagining_.
  - This takes effect at the start of the next session, and the new mod needs to be designed/created.
